# Why Neurobrain X?

## The Problem
Modern Security Operations Centers (SOCs) are drowning in alerts. Attackers routinely evade detection by using valid credentials, rotating IPs, and moving laterally through networks. The result is "alert fatigue"—analysts manually stitching together isolated events, often missing the broader campaign until the damage is done.

## The Innovation
Neurobrain X introduces **Deterministic Gap Inference**. 
Instead of relying on fragile rule sets or hallucination-prone LLMs, our engine constructs a **Dynamic Security Graph** from raw telemetry. By fusing spatial context (building floors, network segments) with a robust Identity Continuity Layer, we can mathematically infer the missing links between disconnected alerts. 

## The Architecture
Our pipeline is 100% deterministic:
1. **AttackReconstructor**: Connects the dots across missing telemetry.
2. **Identity Layer**: Penalizes identity spoofing and rewards session continuity.
3. **SOC Copilot & Response Engine**: Maps the final structural graph to actionable, severity-based mitigations using MITRE ATT&CK.

## Industry Impact
Neurobrain X reduces Mean Time to Respond (MTTR) by automating the most labor-intensive part of a SOC analyst's job: the investigation. We deliver the complete narrative of an attack before the analyst even opens the ticket.
