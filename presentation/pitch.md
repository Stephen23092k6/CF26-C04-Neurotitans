"Every day, analysts spend hours playing connect-the-dots with cybersecurity alerts. By the time they see the full picture, the ransomware has already detonated. Neurobrain X connects the dots for them, autonomously, in milliseconds."

**The Solution:**
"Neurobrain X is an Autonomous Cyber Defense Command Center. It ingests disconnected network and identity telemetry, infers the missing gaps where attackers try to hide, and outputs a complete, deterministic timeline of the attack."

**The Secret Sauce:**
"Unlike generative AI solutions that hallucinate investigations, our engine uses strict, graph-based structural inference. It maps physical building locations, network topology, and identity continuity to mathematically prove an attack path exists."

**The Ask/Close:**
"With Neurobrain X, we don't just alert SOC teams—we give them the complete story and the exact buttons to push to stop it. This is the future of autonomous defense."
